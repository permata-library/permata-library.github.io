    Free e-books.

    Legal places to read for free when a book isn't on our shelves.

    • u-Pustaka

      Malaysia's National Library e-lending. Free with a PNM membership, in BM and English.

      Open
    • OpenStax

      Free textbooks for Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Calculus and Statistics. Matches most AP courses.

      Open
    • Project Gutenberg

      Tens of thousands of classic books whose copyright has ended.

      Open
    • Standard Ebooks

      Classics, carefully formatted for phones and e-readers.

      Open
    • Open Library

      Borrow e-books for free with an account, like a normal library.

      Open
    • DOAB

      Academic books published free by their authors and universities.

      Open

    Why only these?

    Every site here shares books with the author's or publisher's permission. Sites that copy books without permission break the Copyright Act 1987, so the library can't link to them.
